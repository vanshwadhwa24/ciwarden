Sparse Retrieval for Handwritten Field Notes: A Small Pilot

Tomasz Ellery, Wren Achebe-Marsh. Department of Applied Computing, Halden Institute (fictional).

Abstract

We test a sparse retrieval method on 412 scanned field notebooks. On our small benchmark it improves recall at 10 from 0.61 to 0.64 over a BM25 baseline. The gain is within the confidence interval on two of three splits. We discuss limits, including the size of the dataset and the single annotator.
